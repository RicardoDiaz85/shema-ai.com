Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Jewish context important for Bible study?

Jewish context reveals the rabbinic meaning behind Jesus’ teachings, the Hebrew imagery, idioms, and customs woven into Scripture. Western translations often skim the surface, missing parables, remez (hidden clues), and everyday expressions familiar to His Jewish audience. Studying Scripture this way brings depth, clarity, and transformation to your faith.

👉 Learn more about the Eastern context often missed in translation

How does SHEMA’s AI provide Jewish commentary and insight?

SHEMA’s AI is trained on trusted Messianic Jewish sources, rabbinic commentary, and scholarly insight. It delivers verse-by-verse context, highlighting language, history, and culture so you can see Scripture through Jewish eyes.

What makes SHEMA different from other Bible apps?

SHEMA takes you deeper—rooted in the Jewish world of Jesus, not Western categories. By placing heavy weight on the culture, idioms, and honor–shame values of His time, SHEMA helps you hear Scripture as His disciples first heard it.
